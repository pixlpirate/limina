## Changelog

### v1.0.0 `2026-08-25`

- 3D landscape drawn from elevation data, from any viewpoint on Earth
- Visible summits named, with those hidden behind the terrain left out
- Sun, moon and Milky Way paths, timed against the real skyline
- Camera mode, laying the drawing over the landscape in front of you
- Offline storage, so a saved area works with no connection
