## About

[Built and maintained by Jonathan Juste](https://pixelpirate.fr)

It collects **no personal data** and keeps _no history_. Everything is stored on your device, under `localStorage`.

### How to contribute

The app is open source. Corrections and improvements are welcome on the repository.

[pixlpirate/limina](https://github.com/pixlpirate/limina)

#### Leave a tip

No tips here. You can make the world literally better by contributing to OpenStreetMap instead.

- Add summits: [openstreetmap.org](https://www.openstreetmap.org)
- Contribute by playing: [streetcomplete.app](https://streetcomplete.app)
- Or by solving puzzles: [maproulette.org](https://maproulette.org)
- Contribute by learning: [learnosm.org](https://learnosm.org)
- Contribute financially: [osmfoundation.org](https://osmfoundation.org)
